This Cancellation Policy defines the conditions under which orders may be cancelled by customers or by the company, along with applicable timelines and refund handling.

---

## 1. Scope of Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to:

* Physical products ordered through the platform
* Services that explicitly allow cancellation

This policy does **not** apply to:

* Digital products once access is granted
* Customized or made-to-order items
* Products marked as “Non-Cancellable”

---

## 2. Cancellation by Customer

### 2.1 Pre-Shipment Cancellation

Customers may cancel an order **before it is shipped** by:

* Logging into their account
* Navigating to **My Orders**
* Selecting the order and choosing “Cancel”

Pre-shipment cancellations are **free of charge**.

---

### 2.2 Post-Shipment Cancellation

Once an order has been shipped:

* Cancellation is **not permitted**
* Customers must follow the **Return and Refund Policy** instead

Shipment status is final once updated by the logistics partner.

---

## 3. Cancellation by Company

The company reserves the right to cancel an order under the following circumstances:

* Product is out of stock
* Pricing or listing errors are identified
* Payment verification fails
* Suspected fraudulent or unauthorized transactions
* Regulatory or compliance restrictions
* Delivery location is non-serviceable

Customers are notified promptly if an order is cancelled by the company.

---

## 4. Refunds for Cancelled Orders

### 4.1 Customer-Initiated Cancellation (Pre-Shipment)

* Full refund is issued to the original payment method
* Refund initiation occurs within **3–5 business days**

---

### 4.2 Company-Initiated Cancellation

* Full refund is issued automatically
* Refund is processed regardless of payment method used

---

## 5. Partial Cancellations

For orders containing multiple items:

* Customers may cancel individual items **before shipment**
* Shipped items cannot be partially cancelled

Refunds apply only to cancelled items.

---

## 6. Cancellation of Services

Cancellation terms for services vary and are:

* Clearly mentioned on the service detail page
* Subject to service-specific timelines and conditions

Service cancellations outside the allowed window may not be eligible for refunds.

---

## 7. Non-Cancellable Orders

Orders cannot be cancelled if:

* The order status is “Shipped” or “Out for Delivery”
* The product is customized or personalized
* The product is labeled as non-cancellable

Such orders must follow return or replacement procedures if applicable.

---

## 8. Failed Delivery Cancellations

If an order is returned due to:

* Multiple failed delivery attempts
* Incorrect address provided by the customer

The order may be cancelled automatically.

Refund eligibility depends on:

* Product type
* Return condition
* Reason for delivery failure

Shipping charges may be deducted where applicable.

---

## 9. Cancellation Confirmation

Upon successful cancellation:

* A confirmation message is sent via email or SMS
* Order status is updated in the user account

Lack of confirmation indicates the cancellation was not completed.

---

## 10. Disputes Related to Cancellation

Customers may raise disputes if:

* Refund is not received within the stated timeline
* Cancellation request was incorrectly rejected

Disputes must be raised within **14 days** of the cancellation decision.

---

## 11. Abuse of Cancellation Policy

The company reserves the right to:

* Restrict cancellation privileges
* Suspend accounts exhibiting repeated misuse or abuse

Such actions are taken after internal review.

---

## 12. Policy Modifications

The company may:

* Update this Cancellation Policy at any time
* Apply revised policies to future orders

The latest version is always available on the platform.

---
