
This Refund Policy outlines the conditions, timelines, and procedures under which refunds are issued for purchases made on the platform. By placing an order, customers agree to the terms described below.

---

## 1. Scope of the Refund Policy

This policy applies to:

* Physical products purchased through the platform
* Select services explicitly marked as refundable

This policy does **not** apply to:

* Digital goods once accessed or downloaded
* Gift cards, vouchers, or promotional credits
* Items explicitly labeled as **“Non-Refundable”**

---

## 2. Eligibility Criteria for Refund

A refund request may be approved if **any one** of the following conditions is met:

### 2.1 Defective or Damaged Items

* The product is physically damaged at the time of delivery
* The product has manufacturing defects affecting usability

Evidence such as photos or videos may be required.

---

### 2.2 Incorrect Item Delivered

* A different product than what was ordered is delivered
* Incorrect size, color, or variant is delivered

The item must remain unused and in original condition.

---

### 2.3 Item Not as Described

* The product significantly differs from the description on the product page
* Key features or specifications are missing or incorrect

Minor variations in color or packaging do not qualify.

---

### 2.4 Non-Delivery or Late Delivery

* The order is not delivered within the promised delivery timeline
* The shipment is marked delivered but not received by the customer

Verification with the logistics partner may be required.

---

## 3. Non-Refundable Scenarios

Refunds will **not** be issued in the following cases:

* Change of mind after delivery
* Incorrect address provided by the customer
* Damage caused by misuse, negligence, or improper handling
* Products returned without original packaging or accessories
* Requests raised after the eligible refund window

---

## 4. Refund Request Timeline

Customers must initiate a refund request within the following periods:

 Standard products    : Within **7 days** of delivery    
 Perishable / consumable items : Within **48 hours** of delivery  
 Services  : As specified on the service page 

Requests submitted outside these timelines may be automatically rejected.

---

## 5. Refund Request Process

To request a refund, customers must:

1. Log in to their account
2. Navigate to **My Orders**
3. Select the relevant order
4. Choose the reason for refund
5. Submit required supporting evidence (if applicable)

Once submitted, the request enters review status.

---

## 6. Review and Approval Process

* Refund requests are reviewed within **2–4 business days**
* Additional verification may be requested
* The company reserves the right to approve or reject requests based on policy compliance

Decisions are communicated via email or in-app notification.

---

## 7. Refund Method

Approved refunds are processed using one of the following methods:

* Original payment method (preferred)
* Store credit (if original method is unavailable or expired)

Refunds **cannot** be redirected to a different bank account or payment method upon request.

---

## 8. Refund Processing Time

After approval:

* Refunds are initiated within **5–7 business days**
* Bank or payment provider processing may take an additional **3–5 business days**

The company is not responsible for delays caused by banks or payment gateways.

---

## 9. Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued in cases where:

* Only part of the order qualifies for refund
* Returned items show signs of use
* Accessories, manuals, or packaging are missing

The refund amount is determined after inspection.

---

## 10. Inspection of Returned Items

For returns requiring physical pickup:

* Items undergo inspection upon receipt
* Refund approval is contingent on inspection outcome
* Inspection failure may result in refund rejection or reduced refund

---

## 11. Rejected Refund Requests

If a refund request is rejected:

* The reason for rejection will be communicated
* The item may be returned to the customer
* No refund will be processed

---

## 12. Dispute Resolution

Customers may dispute a refund decision by:

* Contacting customer support within **14 days** of rejection
* Providing additional clarification or evidence

Decisions after dispute review are final.

---

## 13. Policy Modifications

The company reserves the right to:

* Modify this Refund Policy at any time
* Apply updated terms to future purchases

The latest version will always be available on the platform.

---
