

This Shipping Policy describes the order processing timelines, delivery methods, charges, and conditions governing the shipment of products purchased on the platform.

---

## 1. Shipping Coverage

### 1.1 Domestic Shipping

We ship products to all **serviceable domestic locations** supported by our logistics partners.

Delivery availability depends on:

* Product type
* Delivery address
* Pin code serviceability

---

### 1.2 International Shipping

International shipping is available only for:

* Selected products
* Selected countries

International shipping availability is displayed on the product page or at checkout.

---

## 2. Order Processing Time

* Orders are processed within **24–48 business hours** after successful payment confirmation.
* Orders placed on **weekends or public holidays** are processed on the next business day.
* Processing time does **not** include shipping or delivery time.

Orders requiring manual verification or customization may take longer.

---

## 3. Shipping Methods

Available shipping methods may include:

* Standard Shipping
* Express Shipping (if supported for the location and product)

The available method and estimated delivery timeline are shown during checkout.

---

## 4. Estimated Delivery Time

Estimated delivery timelines are as follows:

 Standard Shipping is done in **3–7 business days** 
 Express Shipping is done in **1–3 business days** 

Delivery timelines are **estimates only** and may vary due to external factors.

---

## 5. Shipping Charges

* Shipping charges, if applicable, are clearly displayed at checkout before payment.
* Free shipping may be offered:

  * On orders above a specified value
  * During promotional periods

Shipping charges are **non-refundable** once the order is shipped.

---

## 6. Shipment Tracking

* Once an order is shipped, a **tracking ID** is generated.
* Tracking details are shared via:

  * Email
  * SMS
  * Order details page

Tracking updates depend on the logistics partner and may not be real-time.

---

## 7. Delivery Attempts

* Delivery partners typically attempt delivery **up to 2–3 times**.
* If delivery fails due to:

  * Customer unavailability
  * Incorrect address
  * Incomplete contact details

The shipment may be returned to origin.

---

## 8. Delayed Deliveries

Delays may occur due to:

* Weather conditions
* Natural disasters
* Logistics disruptions
* Regulatory restrictions
* High demand periods (sales, festivals)

The company is not liable for delays caused by factors beyond its control.

---

## 9. Undelivered or Lost Shipments

### 9.1 Undelivered Orders

If an order is not delivered within the estimated timeframe:

* Customers may contact support
* An investigation with the logistics partner is initiated

---

### 9.2 Lost Shipments

If a shipment is confirmed as lost:

* A replacement or refund may be issued
* Resolution is subject to verification and internal review

---

## 10. Delivery Confirmation

Delivery is considered complete when:

* The product is marked as delivered by the logistics partner
* The product is handed over at the delivery address

The company is not responsible for loss after confirmed delivery.

---

## 11. Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing:

* Correct delivery address
* Accurate contact details

Orders delivered to incorrect addresses due to customer error are not eligible for refunds.

---

## 12. Damaged Packages During Delivery

If the outer package appears damaged:

* Customers are advised to inspect before accepting delivery
* Any visible damage should be reported immediately

Acceptance of delivery indicates the package was received in acceptable condition.

---

## 13. Shipping Restrictions

Certain products may be subject to:

* Regional restrictions
* Regulatory shipping limitations
* Carrier-specific constraints

Such restrictions are communicated at checkout where applicable.

---

## 14. Policy Changes

The company reserves the right to:

* Update or modify this Shipping Policy at any time
* Apply revised terms to future orders

The latest version will be published on the platform.

---
